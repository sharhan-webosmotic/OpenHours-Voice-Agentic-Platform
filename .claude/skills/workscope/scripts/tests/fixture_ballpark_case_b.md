# 1. Overview

Harvestly named its MVP: farm stock listing and buyer orders, with reporting and a mobile app to follow.

# 2. What We Will Build

## MVP

1. **Stock Listing**
   - **Daily stock** entered by each farm
2. **Buyer Orders**
   - **Order and confirm** from the listing

## Phase 2: Reporting *(tentative)*

1. **Sales Reports**
   - **Weekly report** per farm and buyer

## Phase 3: Mobile App *(tentative)*

1. **Farm Mobile App**
   - **Stock entry** from the field
